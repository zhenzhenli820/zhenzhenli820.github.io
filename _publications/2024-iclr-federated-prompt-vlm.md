## Abstract
FedTPG learns a single, unified prompt-generation network shared across many federated clients, rather than learning per-client prompt vectors as in prior federated prompt-learning work. Because prompts are conditioned on task-related text, they stay context-aware and generalize better to both unseen classes and unseen datasets.

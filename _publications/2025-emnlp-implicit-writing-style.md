## Abstract
Evaluates whether LLMs can imitate an individual's implicit writing style from few-shot examples, testing over 40,000 generations across 400+ real authors in news, email, forum, and blog domains. Finds LLMs approximate style reasonably well in structured genres (news, email) but struggle in informal, nuanced writing (blogs, forums), per authorship-attribution, verification, stylistic, and AI-detection evaluations.

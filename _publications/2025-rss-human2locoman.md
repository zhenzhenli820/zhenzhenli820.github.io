## Abstract
Introduces a cross-embodiment imitation-learning pipeline for the LocoMan quadruped, pretraining a Modularized Cross-Embodiment Transformer on cheap human demonstrations (collected via an XR headset), then fine-tuning on limited robot teleoperation data. Reports 41.9% overall and 79.7% out-of-distribution success-rate improvements over baselines, matching baseline performance using half the robot data thanks to human pretraining.
